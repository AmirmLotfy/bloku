# Changelog

## 2.0.0

- Free ad blocking and distraction control with no account, subscription or paid feature tier.
- Packaged AdGuard filters, optional regional and annoyance lists, and per-site ad pauses.
- Distraction presets, saved element hides and restore controls.
- Focus sessions, weekly schedules and daily site allowances.
- Optional supported cookie-rejection flows, clicked-link cleanup and a limited DOM privacy preference.
- Local settings import/export, local activity controls and migration of earlier settings.
- Eight interface languages, Arabic RTL, and light/dark themes.
- Localized static website with help, privacy and product pages.

Coverage depends on the site and browser capacity. The privacy preference does not send the `Sec-GPC` header. See the [extension guide](extension/README.md) and [security policy](SECURITY.md) for limitations.
